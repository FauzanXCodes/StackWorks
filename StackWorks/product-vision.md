Product Vision Build a comprehensive education technology ecosystem that brings educational institutions, educators, and learners together on one platform. The platform should serve three primary groups:
Schools Provide schools with a centralized platform to manage their day-to-day academic and administrative operations. The platform should eventually cover:
Student management
Teacher and staff management
Classes and sections
Subjects
Attendance
Timetables
Examinations
Results and report cards
Fees
Assignments and homework
Notices and communication
Parent access
Reports and analytics
The goal is to reduce dependence on paper records, spreadsheets, disconnected systems, and manual administrative processes.
2. Universities, Colleges & Engineering Institutes
Provide higher-education institutions with tools designed around their more complex academic structures.
The platform should eventually support:
Student management
Faculty management
Departments
Programs
Courses
Academic years
Semesters
Subjects
Credits
Attendance
Timetables
Internal assessments
Examinations
Marks and results
SGPA/CGPA
Assignments
Fees
Notices and communication
Faculty workload
Projects
Internships
Placements
Institutional analytics
The system should be flexible enough to accommodate different types of universities and colleges rather than forcing every institution into the same academic structure.
3. Students & Individual Learners
Create a separate learning ecosystem for students and individuals who want to develop knowledge and skills.
Learners should eventually be able to:
Discover learning opportunities
Access free courses
Learn through video lessons
Use curated YouTube educational content
Answer questions related to lessons
Practice concepts
Take assessments
Track learning progress
Identify strengths and weaknesses
Develop practical skills
Earn certificates where appropriate
The learning experience should focus on learning + practice + assessment, rather than simply watching videos.
The Bigger Vision
The long-term goal is to connect the entire education journey:
Institution → Educator → Student → Learning → Assessment → Progress → Skills
An institution should be able to manage its operations.
An educator should be able to teach and assess students.
A student should be able to manage their academic life while also accessing additional learning opportunities and developing skills beyond their institution's curriculum.
One Connected Ecosystem
The platform should not be limited to one type of educational institution.
It should be capable of supporting:
Schools → Colleges → Universities → Individual Learners
while allowing each type of user to have an experience designed specifically for their needs.
The underlying ecosystem should be unified, but the functionality presented to each institution or user should be tailored to their context.
Long-Term Goal
Build an education platform where a student does not need completely separate systems for:
Managing their academic life
Communicating with their institution
Accessing educational content
Practicing
Taking assessments
Tracking progress
Developing additional skills
At the same time, institutions should have a reliable platform for managing their academic and administrative operations. The ultimate objective is to create a single digital ecosystem for managing education and enabling learning.
